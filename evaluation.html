Evaluation mapping

Technical context for product security planning.

IIST can help qualified customers understand how hardware-rooted trust functions fit into integration review, SESIP-related resources, and CRA application mapping discussions.

Why mapping matters

Hardware trust has to fit the product architecture.

A secure component is only one part of a secure product. Connected-device manufacturers also consider software lifecycle, updates, vulnerability handling, documentation, deployment process, supply chain, and product claims.

IIST's role is to provide technical context around where hardware-rooted trust functions may help. The customer product, process, and market requirements remain the deciding factors.

Scope: Final CRA conformity depends on the complete customer product, software, lifecycle process, vulnerability handling, documentation, operating environment, and market requirements.

SESIP resources

SESIP-related resources for qualified discussions.

IIST maintains SESIP-related evaluation resources for its hardware trust platform. These resources can support OEM security review and architecture discussion.

For SASE01EPZS, public SESIP Security Target resources identify the platform at a high level as a secure co-processor platform for embedded systems.

How to use them

Use SESIP-related resources as technical input for integration review and mapping discussions. They are not a substitute for evaluating the customer's complete product.
